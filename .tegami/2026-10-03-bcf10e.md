### Detect immediate bound optional-service unwraps

- Report adjacent single-use `const` serviceOption bindings unconditionally consumed by imported `Option.getOrThrow` or
  `getOrThrowWith`, while preserving guarded and genuinely optional consumers.
- Recognize direct point-free pipe unwraps and skip shadowed API bindings. Keep diagnostics free of automatic fixes because
  acquiring a required service changes Effect requirements and absence-error behavior.
