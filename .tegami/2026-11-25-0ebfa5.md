### Add the opt-in `no-non-ascii` rule

- Enable `2digits/no-non-ascii` to report literal non-ASCII characters in code, comments, strings, and JSX. Its message steers towards real UI components instead of text glyphs for icons, controls, and indicators.
- The rule is off by default, so the default lint policy is unchanged.
