### Detect direct asynchronous constructors under `Effect.runSync`

Added the opt-in `2digits/no-async-constructor-in-run-sync` diagnostic for directly visible Promise-backed effects and
positive literal sleep/delay. The rule verifies import bindings and skips callbacks, timeouts, custom Clock providers,
unknown operators and indirect inputs. It provides no autofix because changing runners changes the caller's return type.
