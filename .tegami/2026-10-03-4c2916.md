### Detect repeated fresh layer factories within one graph

Add the opt-in `2digits/no-duplicate-fresh-layer-factory` rule for binding-proven same-file factories and stable arguments
inside a visible Layer composition. Diagnostics identify the earlier construction and explain manual sharing within the
graph owner. Imported factories and unproved cases are skipped; the rule stays out of recommended presets and emits no fix.
