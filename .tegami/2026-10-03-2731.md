### Diagnose ambiguous extracted template literal captures

Added opt-in `2digits/no-ambiguous-template-literal-captures` for adjacent naked Effect Schema String parts when visible
local decoding consumes both captures. The rule respects import aliases, shadowing and literal tuple slots, and leaves
validation-only, discarded and ignored-capture uses clean. Choose a domain separator or constrained part where both
fields matter; no delimiter autofix is offered. The rule is excluded from `recommendedRules`.
