### Flag unguarded `JSON.parse` in Effect mapping callbacks

- Added a parser diagnostic to `no-throw-in-effect-callback` for global `JSON.parse` calls, direct mappers, and exact immutable local parser aliases.

- Kept parsing fixes manual so applications can choose their own typed error contract.
