### Detect unused Effect callback completion parameters

Extended `no-empty-effect-callback` to report nonempty registrations with an unreferenced completion binding, including
Effect import aliases. The new diagnostic has no automatic fix and does not claim all-path noncompletion. Existing
empty-body fixes and recommended-rule policy are unchanged; intentional completion-free adapters can retain their
registration and cleanup with a documented suppression.
