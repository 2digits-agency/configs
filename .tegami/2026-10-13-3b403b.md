### Report duration-like constants in `prefer-effect-duration`

The rule now also reports `const` declarations in Effect files whose name advertises a duration — a unit such as `MS`, `Seconds`, or `Minutes`, or a concept such as `timeout`, `delay`, `interval`, `ttl`, `timeToLive`, or `backoff` — while the initializer is a bare number or a multiplication of numbers and identifiers, for example `TIMEOUT_MS = 5000` or `BACKOFF_MS = BASE_MS * 2`. The diagnostic recommends the `Duration` constructor matching the advertised unit and `Duration.toMillis(name)` where a plain millisecond number is required.

Constants get no autofix, because they can also feed number-typed APIs such as `setTimeout`. Files without an Effect import, mutable bindings, aliases, computed initializers, zero or negative sentinel values, counts such as `TIMEOUT_COUNT`, and constants already annotated `Duration` or `Duration.Input` stay unreported.

`@2digits/oxlint-config` picks the change up through `recommendedRules`, so no config change is needed.
