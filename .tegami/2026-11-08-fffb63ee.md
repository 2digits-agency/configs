### Ban `@ts-ignore` and require meaningful `@ts-expect-error` descriptions

`ts/ban-ts-comment` (ESLint) and `typescript/ban-ts-comment` (Oxlint) are stricter. This can report new errors in existing code.

- `@ts-ignore` is no longer allowed, even with a description. Use `@ts-expect-error`, which fails once the error it suppresses is gone. ESLint offers a suggestion to replace it.
- `@ts-expect-error` needs a description of at least 10 characters, counted after the directive, for example `// @ts-expect-error: upstream types omit ref`. Before, 3 characters were enough.
