### Add an opt-in diagnostic for dynamic dictionary prototype collisions

Added `2digits/no-unsafe-dynamic-record-key` for provable open-string writes and presence/fallback reads on explicitly
typed, same-file ordinary-object dictionaries. Own-key guards protect reads, not unrestricted writes. The rule offers
no automatic fixes and stays outside the recommended preset pending broader precision evidence.
